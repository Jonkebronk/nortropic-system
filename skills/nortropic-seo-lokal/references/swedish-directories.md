## Tier 3 — auto-listings to VERIFY (not create)
allabolag.se, merinfo.se, proff.se, ratsit.se, bolagsfakta.se — generated from Bolagsverket/SCB. Check: correct address after any move, correct SNI code (affects category display), website URL present where editable. Wrong data here contradicts your NAP silently.

## Omdömen playbook
1. **Direct review link**: GBP → "Ask for reviews" → short link. Put it: on the faktura (QR), in the job-done SMS, in email signature
2. Ask at the moment of delight (job finished, problem solved) — not days later
3. Respond to EVERY review in Swedish within 7 days. Negative ones: factual, calm, offer to resolve offline — the response is marketing to the next reader
4. Never incentivize or gate reviews (both against Google policy and marknadsföringslagen risk)
5. Site displays: real aggregate + 3 named quotes (namn + ort), linked "Läs alla omdömen på Google"

## Quarterly audit (15 min)
- Search `"[företagsnamn]" [stad]` — check top 20 results for stale NAP
- Google the phone number in quotes — finds directories with the number attached to old/wrong entries
- Fix at the source directory, never ignore ("citation drift" erodes local rank slowly)
